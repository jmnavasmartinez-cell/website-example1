Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most.

How often should I get a cleaning?

For most people, every six months. If you have sensitive gums or wear braces, we'll recommend a different schedule after your assessment.

Does whitening hurt?

It shouldn't. Some temporary sensitivity can appear for a day or two, and we use desensitizing gels to reduce it.

Do you handle emergencies?

Yes. If you have severe pain, swelling or a broken tooth, call us and we'll give you the first free slot of the day.

Can I pay in stages?

For long treatments such as orthodontics, we split the payment into stages. Everything is agreed in writing from the start.
